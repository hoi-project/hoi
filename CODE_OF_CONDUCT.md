# Code of conduct

hoi adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone
represents the project.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, critique work and not people, and accept that a
maintainer may decline a change. Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers at [hello@hoi.sh](mailto:hello@hoi.sh). Do not open a public
issue about another person. Reports are kept confidential; the maintainers will reply, review what
happened and decide on a response, from a warning to a permanent ban, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
