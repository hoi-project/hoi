4 · show the receipts

What actually happened?

Read what residents chose and what the protocol accepted. A proposal, a hide request, or a membership objection is an attempt; only observed state changes show that it took effect.

The link exercise only measures interaction with an intercepted local link. It cannot establish whether arbitrary links are safe. A quiet run or an unsuccessful takeover is not proof of security.
