Network playground

Pick what you are and how you come in, press Join, and watch your device find its way into a network of thousands: which pipe opens to whom, which frame crosses it, what each side keeps. The network is simulated in this tab; the frames, fields, limits and timings are the real protocol's, each linked to the code that defines it.

      Needs JavaScript

      The playground draws and runs in your browser. Every path it plays is written out, frame by frame, on Joining, step by step.

      Drag to pan, pinch or use + and − to zoom, and Fit to see the whole network. Tap a device or a step to read its details. Use the views to switch between the network, setup, details and activity.

      Reading the picture

      The picture is a floor plan. Each room is a part of the network; each pipe is a connection; the dots flowing through the pipes are messages, colored by what they carry. A handful of devices stand for each kind, and the round tanks hold the rest: their numbers drift as a live network's would.

      What's real here

      realfrom the code

      The order of the steps, every message name and field, the link formats, the transports, ports, ALPNs, caps, timeouts and retry intervals. Each step cites the files and lines that define it.

      illustrativeshaped like the real thing

      Example values: keys, addresses (from the documentation ranges 203.0.113.0/24 and 198.51.100.0/24), counts of records and listings.

      simulatedmade up

      The size of the network, the traffic in the background, and the timing on screen (sped up so a step takes a second, not a 15-second timeout).

      Joining, step by stepThe same paths, written out with every stored field. TransportsQUIC, WebRTC, the WebSocket link and relays. Sync & gossiphello, rng, want, recs and the rest. DiscoveryNode lists, catalogs, the DHT and LAN beacons.
