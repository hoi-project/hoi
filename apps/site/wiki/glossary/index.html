Glossary

The words hoi uses, each in a sentence or two, with a link to where it is explained in full. Names in code are the code's own.

A – C

admission

How a newcomer becomes a member of a community: by enough vouches from established members, a moderator's admit ledger entry, or (where a community requires it) an anonymous age proof. Communities.

aggregator

A device that counts a community's votes and publishes the counts as a signed tally record. People follow aggregators as they follow labelers; their counts never feed ranking on their own. Trust & ranking.

all-or-nothing transformAONT

A step in scattered delivery that makes a sealed object unreadable unless every one of its bytes is present, so fewer pieces than needed reveal nothing. Scattered delivery.

Argon2id

The memory-hard function behind identity work and the keystore. Stamps & work.

autoban

A device banning a key in a community once weighted reports about it reach the community's autoban_threshold (12 by default). Spam & rate limits.

bind

The message a node sends first on a WebRTC channel a browser dialed: a signature over both certificate fingerprints, the ICE username and the node's endpoint, proving the channel reaches the key the route named. Transports.

BLAKE3

The hash behind record ids, stamps, key derivation and most labels. Records & encoding.

blob

The bytes of a media item, which travel separately from the record that names them by hash: bwant and blob messages, or iroh-blobs between native nodes. Media.

blocklist

A signed list of keys a person publishes so others can follow it. Trust & ranking.

bounce

One device's own verdict on a key: it stops accepting the key's records for a while. The first bounce lasts a day, repeats double, up to 30 days. Spam & rate limits.

burn

A revoke whose device is the root itself: every device that takes it in drops everything the person signed and refuses what they sign later. No undo. Keys & identity.

bye

The message that ends a link, with a reason. Sync & gossip.

canonical DAG-CBOR

The one byte encoding every record has, so the same record always has the same id and signature. The decoder refuses anything not encoded exactly that way. Records & encoding.

carry, carrier

A public node keeping an open community for others while its members are away, after someone sends it the signed genesis (carry) and it agrees (carried). Discovery.

catalog

The message in which a node lists the open communities it carries and the listings it heard of. Discovery.

changes by consent

New rules and limits are proposals that the people established at the time decide on, with signed reviews; silence counts as yes after a fade window. Communities.

community

A key and the records it governs. Its first record, the genesis, names its owners, their signature threshold and its limits. Communities.

consent gate

In browsers, desktops and phones, the rule that a device says nothing to a public node until its person has read and accepted that node's operator notice. Headless nodes have none. Discovery.

contest window

How long a removed record naps, readable and vetoable, before its body is deleted: 72 hours by default, at most 14 days. Communities.

D – H

delegation

The record that makes a device key speak for a root: signed by the device (its consent) and blessed by the root, with a scope and an expiry. Keys & identity.

DHT

The Mainline DHT that public nodes run among themselves with no outside bootstrap, announcing the open communities they carry. Discovery.

did:key

How a public key is written as an identifier. Records & encoding.

direct link

A WebRTC channel between two browsers, introduced by a node that relays their offer and answer (here, sig). Opt-in, public communities only. Joining.

directory

A device's list of open communities it has heard of, with their signed definitions and join routes, spread by catalog gossip. Discovery.

dispatch

Node::call: the core's single entry point for interfaces, a method name and JSON arguments in, JSON out. Core API.

encrypted relay

An optional HTTPS service holding padded ciphertext for private communities, by room, for at most 31 days. It never sees member keys or community ids. Mailboxes & relays.

endpoint

A native node's iroh key, which names it on the network. Separate from any person's posting key. Transports.

epoch

One state of a private community's MLS group. Every commit starts a new one, and the rooms and keys a carrier can see are derived from it. Private communities.

established

The third rung: a member for long enough (14 days by default). Their vouches, reviews and vetoes count. Standing & review.

expires_at

When a record fades, no later than the community's retention allows. Past it, devices delete it and leave it out of sync. Expiry & removal.

featuresfx

The list in a hello saying what a device offers: range, carry, find, dht, signal, unlisted and more. Sync & gossip.

find, found

Asking public nodes for routes to a community no connected peer serves, and their answer. Discovery.

genesis

A community's first record, the only one its community key signs. Communities.

gossip

New records pushed in recs to connected peers as they arrive. Sync & gossip.

hello

The first message on every link: version, node key, communities (named or salted), routes and features. Sync & gossip.

hide

An established member's signed judgment that a post breaks a rule; enough voice puts the post to sleep for everyone. Standing & review.

hold

What a device does with a record beyond its author's standing: store it, show it only to its author, pass it to nobody. Standing & review.

holder

A node that keeps pieces for others and answers sealed requests; mailboxes and pickups use them. Scattered delivery.

home node

Your own desktop or hoi-node, serving your other devices over a local WebSocket or WebRTC. hoi-node.

I – M

identity work

A one-time Argon2id proof over a key, so making many keys costs many times the work. Each community says how much it asks for. Stamps & work.

invite link

A hoi://join link carrying a community key and routes to reach it, and often its genesis. Links & URIs.

iroh

The QUIC library native nodes use, with ALPN hoi/sync/1 and none of its operator's default services. Transports.

labeler

A key whose labels a person chooses to trust and apply to what they see. Trust & ranking.

ladder

The four rungs of standing (newcomer, member, established, trusted) every device computes from the community's own records. Standing & review.

LAN beacon

A UDP multicast packet (239.255.70.74:7848) a native device sends every 30 s for each public community it joined, sealed so only members can read where to connect. Discovery.

ledger

A community's signed, sequenced list of moderation actions, which every device replays into the community's state. Communities.

linking

Adding a new device to an identity: a sealed request through the home node, four digits compared on both screens, and the root device's approval. Keys & identity.

mailbox

Holders a private group names where members leave sealed messages for each other. Mailboxes & relays.

masked code

The envelope every hoi link carries: magic bytes, a checksum, and the payload XORed with a keystream derived from the checksum, so no address or token is readable in the link itself. Links & URIs.

member

The second rung: admitted. Links allowed, votes count. Standing & review.

MLS

Messaging Layer Security (OpenMLS), the group encryption under private communities. Private communities.

N – R

newcomer

The first rung: a key with enough identity work, not yet admitted. Text only, and few posts a day. Standing & review.

node link

A hoi://node link naming a public node's routes and carrying its operator notice; a browser's way in. Links & URIs.

node listnodes

The message in which a public node passes on the public nodes it reached itself, with the uptime it saw. Discovery.

nonce

The number a miner varies until a record's stamp has enough leading zero bits. Stamps & work.

operator notice

What a public node's operator says about what the node keeps and who runs it, signed by the node and read before connecting. Discovery.

packet relay

An iroh relay a hoi-node can run (--packet-relay): it forwards encrypted QUIC packets by endpoint and keeps only who is connected now. hoi-node.

pairing

Connecting a new device to your home node with a one-time token, swapped for a credential. Joining.

pickup

Where a private invitation waits, sealed to the newcomer's one-time key, on holders the newcomer named. Mailboxes & relays.

pieces

The data and parity shares of a scattered object; any enough of them rebuild it. Scattered delivery.

public node

A node that accepts strangers and carries open communities; the only kind that publishes an address. Discovery.

range reconciliationrng

How two devices learn what the other has by comparing fingerprints of ranges of (created_at, id), splitting the ranges that differ. Sync & gossip.

rate window

How many records of a kind a key may have in a community within an hour or a day, counted by created_at so every device agrees. Spam & rate limits.

record

Everything in hoi: a signed, immutable, canonically encoded piece of data with an author, a kind, a time and an expiry. Records & encoding.

recovery line

A person's master secret written as one line (hoi-key-1-…), from which a new device rebuilds the identity. Keys & identity.

recs, want

Records by their signed bytes, and the request for records by id. Sync & gossip.

Reed–Solomon

The erasure code over GF(2⁸) that makes parity pieces. Scattered delivery.

registry

An optional service mapping names to community keys in a signed, append-only log, and listing short-lived node announcements. Registry.

removal

A moderator's remove ledger entry: the record naps through the contest window, then only its id and reason stay. Communities.

report

A signed record naming a key and record ids as evidence; weighted reports can autoban. Spam & rate limits.

revoke

The record that removes a device from an identity. Keys & identity.

room

What an encrypted relay stores objects under: an id derived from a private group's epoch, meaningless to anyone outside it. Private communities.

root key

The Ed25519 key that is a person. It blesses device keys and never signs posts itself. Keys & identity.

route

An address string: <endpoint>@ip:port for QUIC, rtc:<fingerprint>@ip:port for WebRTC. Discovery.

S – Z

scattered delivery

Sealing an object and spreading it as pieces over many holders so no small set of them can read or withhold it. Scattered delivery.

Shamir's secret sharing

How a master secret is split among friends: any three of five pieces bring it back, two say nothing. Keys & identity.

stamp

About a second of proof of work on every record: BLAKE3(id ‖ nonce) must start with enough zero bits. Stamps & work.

standing

What a key may do in a community, computed by every device from that community's own records. Standing & review.

strike

A device's fading score per key and community; past 10 the key is bounced and a report signed. The score halves every hour. Spam & rate limits.

suppression

A node operator refusing to serve a community, record or blob on their own node. Nothing is signed or sent; other nodes decide for themselves. Trust & ranking.

tally

The vote count behind a post, which each device computes itself, or a signed count from an aggregator. Trust & ranking.

tombstone

What stays of a removed or deleted record: its id and the reason, so it can't come back. Expiry & removal.

tracker

A registry address a community names, where public nodes carrying it announce themselves. Discovery.

trusted

The top rung: an established member with recent contributions the community answered well. Images, audio and video. Fades if they stop. Standing & review.

unlisted

A device that publishes no internet address of its own; it says so in its features. Discovery.

vault

The local store of keys and community data, sealed under a data key that a passphrase or passkey unwraps. Keys & identity.

veto

An established member's objection to a moderator's action; enough voice overturns it on every device. Standing & review.

voice

The weight of a person in hides, blocks, vetoes and objections, handed down through vouches and capped by the strongest voucher. Standing & review.

vouch

An established member's signed word that a newcomer belongs. Standing & review.

warmth

How many other durable places hold a community's live records: cold, cool, warm or hot. Sync & gossip.

WebRTC

How browsers and phones reach nodes (UDP 7844) and, with direct links, each other: a data channel named hoi, ICE-lite, DTLS. Transports.
