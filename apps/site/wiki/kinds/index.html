Record kinds

The code defines 24 kinds of record. They share the envelope described on Records & encoding and differ in their body. For each kind this page gives its purpose, every body field with its CBOR type, limits and optionality, whether it carries a community and a parent, which key may sign it, the checks a receiver applies, its stamp class, how long it lives, and an example decoded from the conformance vectors.

Source hoi-types/src/record.rs hoi-validate/src/lib.rs hoi-validate/src/text.rs hoi-core/src/node.rs vectors/records.json

All kinds at a glance

The kind string is what the wire carries (Kind::as_str). "Community" and "parent" say whether the envelope field is required (✓), forbidden (–) or optional (opt), as check_shape enforces. "Scope" is what a device's delegation must cover to sign it (scope_needed); "root" means only a root (or the community, or a recovery) key signs it. "Stamp" is the stamp class: full is the community's pow_bits, −8 is pow_bits − 8, 16 is the fixed GLOBAL_POW_BITS, before any key-age discount (Stamps & work). "Lives" is the expiry cap: the community's max_retention, or KEEP (100 years) for kinds outside the retention window.

How to read the field tables

  • Types are CBOR types: uint, int (signed), text, bytes, bytes32 (exactly 32 bytes: a key or record id), bytes64 (a signature), […] arrays, {…} maps. Integers typed u32 in Rust refuse values above 232−1.
  • Optional fields are left out when absent; writing an empty default is refused where noted. Unknown fields are always refused.
  • Limits come from three places: the decoder (shape and types), hoi_validate::content (lengths, allowed values, the text rules) and the node's semantic checks (who may write what about what). Character limits count Unicode scalar values. "Line" means one line of plain text checked by text::line; "markdown" means the markdown subset checked by text::markdown, with a character and a newline limit; both reject direction tricks, invisible and smuggled characters (Text, links & names).
  • Hide reasons, wherever a reason is "a hide reason": spam, abuse, illegal, off-topic, or rule:<n> with 1 to 3 digits (is_hide_reason). Disagreement is not one: that is what a downvote is for.
  • Examples are the vectors in records.json, decoded and shown as JSON with byte strings as h'…', keys in canonical order and long values cut. Their stamps are mined at 8 bits and times start at 1,790,000,000.

Posts and replies

The kinds people read. Each is signed by a device key whose delegation covers post or comment (or *), or by a root directly; each pays the community's stamp; each lives at most the community's max_retention. Before publishing, the app also checks the author's standing: links need a member, media a trusted member (Standing & review).

Opens a thread. No parent. The app sets expires_at to created_at + max_retention and name to the author's display name.

An up- or downvote on a post or comment of the same community. Only the highest seq per voter and target counts, so a new vote replaces the last and 0 takes it back. community: the genesis

A community is an Ed25519 key pair, and its first record is its genesis: signed by the community key, with community equal to author, no parent and no community scope (only that key signs it). It is the only record a community key ever signs: the app makes the key, signs the genesis and forgets the key, and a node refuses a second genesis for the same key (a community's genesis never changes). From then on the owners govern through the ledger. It pays the fixed 16-bit stamp and is kept for 100 years (the app sets expires_at to created_at + KEEP).

ledger: moderation and governance

Every moderation action and every change to a community's rules, limits or moderators is a ledger record with a sequence number. Vouches, reviews, labels and reports

The small signed judgements standing and moderation are computed from. All four need the vote scope, pay the −8 stamp class and count in rate groups of their own. Whose of them count, and for what, is decided by the ladder on every device (Standing & review).

vouch

parent is the person (a root key) vouched for; nobody vouches for themselves, which check_shape and the node both refuse. 1 vouches, -1 objects and needs a hide reason, 0 takes it back. The latest (seq, created_at, id) per voucher and person wins and older ones are dropped. Vouches are what standing is built from, so they live outside the retention window (the app writes expires_at = created_at + KEEP). The app also vouches automatically once an established member has read a couple of a newcomer's posts without hiding them.

review

A member's judgement of a record: parent is a post or comment, or a ledger entry, of the same community. The node refuses a review of your own post or comment, of a ledger entry you signed or cosigned, a veto of a post or comment, a hide of a ledger entry, a review of a ledger entry that is neither vetoable nor an open proposal, and an answer to a proposal after its deadline. A signed report against a person: parent is the reported root key and evidence lists record ids, never the posts themselves. Keys: identity, delegation, revoke, rotate, share

The records that make a person. The first four live outside any community, pay the fixed 16-bit stamp and are kept for 100 years; a node takes them from strangers only for roots it already knows, or that arrive in the same batch as their community records. The full story (roots, devices, burning and recovery) is on Keys & identity.

identity

A root key's birth certificate, signed by the root itself. work is the nonce of its memory-hard Argon2id work (Stamps & work); a receiver refuses a weak key and any work under 4 bits, and a community refuses authors whose best identity carries less than its ladder.identity_bits. A device key can't be an identity, and a root can't name itself in of.

delegation

A device blessed by a root. It carries two signatures: the record's own, by the device (its consent, so nobody can claim someone else's device), and root_sig, the root's blessing over delegation_statement(root, device, scope, expires_at) (Records & encoding shows its bytes). Both keys must be strong, the root may not be the device, and body.expires_at must equal the record's expires_at and be at most 400 days after created_at. A device belongs to the first root that blessed it, an identity can't be a device, and a root has at most 16 live devices. The app blesses for 180 days with scope ["*"] and renews before it lapses.

rotate

A recovery key hands a person over to a new root. The signer must be the recovery key whose commitment (rc) an identity along the person's chain of roots carries; to must be a root whose identity names person in of and belongs to nobody else. person, to and the signer must all differ, and a weak recovery key is refused. From the rotation on, the replaced root and its devices sign nothing new.

share

One share of a person's recovery line, given to a friend: parent is the friend's root, and the share is sealed separately to each of the friend's devices. Unlike the other key records it is published in a community, needs the vote scope and pays the −8 class; it is kept for 100 years. The latest seq per author and friend wins, and an empty sealed takes the share back. Rate: 20 a day.

Lists people follow: trust, tally, blocklist

Statements that matter only to people who choose to follow their author. A node takes a tally or a blocklist only from an author this device follows (or from itself); a trust statement is read only by people who trust its author. All three need the vote scope, pay −8, and the latest (created_at, id) per author, community and kind replaces the earlier ones, which are dropped. Every list must be sorted, each entry once (check_shape).

wiki

One edit to a page of a community's wiki, as a Loro update to the page's document. Owners, moderators and admitted members may edit, unless banned or blocked (members edit the wiki). The update must decode as a Loro import blob, and the edits held for one page may total at most 1 MiB (this wiki page is full). A page is its whole history, each edit building on the ones before, so wiki edits are kept for 100 years. The post scope covers it; stamp class −8; rate 60 an hour, 200 a day.

Scattered delivery: pointer and roster

Two kinds serve open communities whose posts travel in pieces (Scattered delivery).

pointer

What syncs in place of a post, comment or edit kept in pieces. It names what it holds in its body, never by parent. Its sealed bytes are the pointer (where the pieces are) sealed under the community's read key, so nodes that never joined can't open it. The post or comment scope covers it, and it pays the community's full stamp. The app sets expires_at to the expiry in the pointer's manifest. Rates: a newcomer 20 an hour and 60 a day; a member 120 an hour and 1,000 a day.
