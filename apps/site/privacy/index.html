Hosting

This website is a static site hosted by {{HOSTER}}. When you visit it, the hoster automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time, the page requested and your browser type in server logs. This is necessary to deliver the website and protect it against abuse. The hoster's privacy statement says what it keeps, for how long, and where in the world it may process that data.

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or third-party content. Fonts, scripts and documentation are served from this domain. The page scripts do not send questions or usage data to us or to an AI service.

Wiki explanations

On supported devices, an optional section helper uses the browser's built-in AI. Chrome downloads and manages its model; questions, documentation excerpts and answers are processed on your device. Opening a helper loads additional documentation from this site. Conversation context stays in memory. When you follow a suggested explanation link, a short handoff is kept in this tab's session storage, consumed at the next wiki page, and accepted for at most 15 minutes. It is not included in links or server requests. Closing the tab clears that storage. There is no cloud AI fallback.

Invite and node links

Invite data and node addresses are placed after the # in a link. Browsers do not send this part to the server, so it does not appear in the hoster's logs.

The client

When you use the hoi client, it connects to a peer-to-peer network that we do not operate: your IP address and connection data reach the nodes and participants you connect with, who may be anywhere in the world and under any law. The client says so before its first connection (Network & Safety) and carries its own privacy notice. Each public node shows its operator's join notice before it serves you; that operator is responsible for the data processed on their node. Operators are asked, not forced, to say who they are and how to reach them; a notice that leaves this out says so.

Not just one node

The node or invite you start with is only the way in. Once you are in, joining communities, opening invites and links, reading, posting, replying and syncing can connect your device to further nodes and participants anywhere on the internet, and public nodes tell it about other public nodes it may connect to on its own. Which ones changes over time. What you publish in an open community is not encrypted and is copied to every node and member that carries it, including nodes your device never connected to.

Running a node

Whoever runs a public node processes other people's data and answers for it; before you run a public node says what that means and what the installer asks of you.

Your rights

You can request information about data concerning you and its correction or deletion via the contact above. For data processed on a node, contact its operator through the contact in its join notice; a node whose operator gave no contact offers no way to ask, and you can decline it.
